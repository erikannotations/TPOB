This repository includes the full metadata of the Thomas Pynchon Bibliography (TPOB), a bibliometric resource for Pynchon studies.

TPOB is introduced in the journal article,

* Erik Ketzan, “A Bibliometric Overview of Pynchon Studies: Introducing the Thomas Pynchon Online Bibliography (TPOB)”. *Orbit: A Journal of American Literature* 12:1 (2025), https://doi.org/10.16995/orbit.23348
* https://orbit.openlibhums.org/article/id/23348/

TPOB is available on Zotero:

* https://www.zotero.org/groups/5470223/thomas_pynchon_online_bibliography/library

To add Pynchon-related articles, books, etc., or to suggest an edit/correction to an existing entry, please use the following form: https://forms.office.com/e/KiX0hQgeWN


**Files:**

Full export of the bibliography in .csv and BibLaTeX formats.

**Updates:**

* October 11 2026: since the initial publication in October 2025, TPOB has grown from 3,489 to 3,693 items: from 155 monographs to 161, 89 edited collections to 92, 927 book chapters and sections to 984, and 2,312 articles to 2,444. New additions include 118 sources in Mandarin Chinese, 5 in Arabic, a Russian edited volume with 11 of its chapters, and 32 publications from 2025–26. We have also improved metadata on existing entries: adding DOIs to 51 records and license information to 57 *Orbit* articles, moving ISBN, DOI, and series data into their proper fields for 53 records, correcting title typos, and removing duplicate records.

* October 11 2026: updated metadata on new Russian edited volume, *Энтропия параболы. Комментарий к роману Томаса Пинчона «Радуга тяготения» / Parabola's Entropy: A Commentary on Thomas Pynchon's Gravity's Rainbow* (2025). Thanks to Stanislav Raspechenko.

* October 11 2026: began maintaining TPOB programmatically via the Zotero API. As a first step, standardized 1,205 language tags to ISO 639-1 codes (e.g., "eng"/"English" → "en", "ger"/"German" → "de", "mandarin" → "zh").

* June 18 2026: following our contribution to [International Pynchon Week 2026](https://anglistik.kuwi.tu-dortmund.de/ipw26/), we added Pynchon studies in Arabic and Mandarin Chinese.

* Erik Ketzan, Manal Altammami, and Xiaorui Yu, "Toward the Thomas Pynchon Online Bibliography 1.5: Mandarin Chinese and Arabic Sources, and the Open Pynchon Reception Corpus", International Pynchon Week 2026, Institut für Sprache, Literatur und Kultur, Technische Universität Dortmund.

* June 18 2026: added 2025/26 publications, corrected license metadata for Orbit articles, updated some other license metadata.
